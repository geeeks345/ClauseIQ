MASTER SAAS CLOUD SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Cloud Services Agreement ("Agreement") is effective as of February 1, 2026 between CloudScale AI Inc ("Vendor") and Enterprise Corp ("Customer").

1. SUBSCRIPTION SERVICES AND SLA
Vendor grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable subscription license to access the CloudScale Platform. Vendor guarantees a 99.9% monthly uptime service level agreement.

2. PAYMENT TERMS AND INVOICING
Fees are invoiced annually in advance. Invoices are payable Net 30 days from date of issuance. Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum statutory limit.

3. EVERGREEN AUTO-RENEWAL AND CANCELLATION
This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms upon expiration of the initial term, unless Customer provides written notice of non-renewal via registered postal mail at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term. Prices upon renewal shall increase by 10% per annum automatically.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION
(a) Cap on Vendor Liability: Under no circumstances shall Vendor's total aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement exceed the fees actually paid by Customer in the preceding three (3) months.
(b) Customer Indemnification: Customer agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Vendor against all third-party claims, liabilities, attorney fees, and consequential damages without financial limitation.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall preserve the confidentiality of the other party's proprietary technical and commercial data for a period of three (3) years from date of disclosure.

6. GOVERNING LAW AND ARBITRATION
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Delaware. All disputes shall be resolved by arbitration under the Commercial Rules of the American Arbitration Association in New York, NY.
